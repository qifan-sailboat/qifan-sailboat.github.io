Security researcher

Qifan Zhang 张起帆

At Palo Alto Networks

I study the security of Internet protocols and the systems that rely on them. My work combines fuzzing, protocol analysis, and formal methods to find vulnerabilities in widely deployed software.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from UC Irvine in 2025, advised by Prof. Zhou Li, and my B.Eng. from ShanghaiTech University in 2020.
