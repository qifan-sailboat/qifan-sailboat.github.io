Mind the Gap: Cross-RFC Formal Analysis Exposes DNSSEC's Hidden Vulnerabilities

Qifan Zhang, Zilin Shen, Imtiaz Karim, Elisa Bertino, Daiping Liu, Zhou Li

Overview

We used SAPIC+ to model how DNSSEC authentication and resolver caching interact across RFCs. The analysis identified an integrity flaw in wildcard handling and two availability flaws. All four affected resolver vendors have released fixes for the wildcard issue.

Cross-Zone Wildcard Cache Poisoning

DNSSECVerif revealed a gap in how DNSSEC resolvers validate wildcards. A signed wildcard from one DNS zone could affect answers for names in another zone. Our Internet-Draft proposes updating RFC 4035 and RFC 8198 to keep wildcard validation within the signing zone. Read Signer-Zone Containment for DNSSEC Wildcard Validation.

I presented the proposed changes to the DNSOP working group at IETF 127. I also presented the formal analysis at DNS-OARC 47 and the Usable Formal Methods Research Group at IETF 127.
