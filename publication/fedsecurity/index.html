FedSecurity: A Benchmark for Attacks and Defenses in Federated Learning and Federated LLMs

Shanshan Han, Baturalp Buyukates, Zijian Hu, Han Jin, Weizhao Jin, Lichao Sun, Xiaoyang Wang, Wenxuan Wu, Chulin Xie, Yuhang Yao, Kai Zhang, Qifan Zhang, Yuhui Zhang, Carlee Joe-Wong, Salman Avestimehr, Chaoyang He

30th ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining, Applied Data Science Track

Preprint PDF Code DOI Cite “

Overview

FedSecurity is a benchmark for studying attacks and defenses in federated learning. Built on FedML, it supplies the training and data-handling infrastructure needed to evaluate security methods across models, datasets, and federated optimizers. Its attacker and defender components support configurable experiments, including federated training of large language models.
